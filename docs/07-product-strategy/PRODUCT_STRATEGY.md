## 1. The promise

**On-brand creative. Client yes. Same place.**

Jigi is the operating system for the last mile of brand creative: from brief or idea, through generation or upload, to a clear, auditable brand decision — without the work dying in email, Slack, or Drive.

Holding-company platforms (WPP Open, Publicis CoreAI / Sapient Bodhi, Omnicom Omni, Dentsu Zoyumi, Havas Converged.AI) have made one thing obvious: **generation without governance is not a product.** The industry is productising *content supply chains* — create → check → approve → activate — with humans firmly in the driver’s seat.

Jigi brings that same loop to the agencies and brands who will never get a custom WPP Open seat or a full Adobe Workfront + GenStudio programme — and who still feel the pain every week.

## 2. The market moment

### Demand is exploding; approval is the bottleneck

Marketers report content demand up roughly fivefold in two years, with another leap expected as personalisation and always-on channels multiply. A single hero idea now becomes dozens of channel variants. Shelf life is short. Teams that cannot approve faster simply fall behind.

Yet creation–approval–activation is routinely described as three to four times longer than it should be. Large organisations admit they cannot see their campaign supply chain end to end. Creatives lose days to chasing comments, hunting the latest file, and guessing whether “LGTM” in Slack counts as brand sign-off.

### What the networks are building (and why it validates us)

| Network | Platform signal | What it proves |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| **WPP** | WPP Open / Open Pro + Brand Guardian | Brands will buy AI that *gates* content for brand, legal, accessibility — then human review |
| **Publicis + Sapient** | CoreAI + Bodhi content supply chain | Create, review, and personalise as one governed system is the enterprise offer |
| **Omnicom** | Omni + Workfront AI Reviewer | “Creativity amplified, not automated”; 25–55% faster production with human oversight |
| **Dentsu** | Zoyumi | Always-on creative with scoring, production, and brand safety in one SaaS — under 30 minutes to on-brand assets |
| **Havas** | Converged.AI + POP | Adobe Workfront/AEM spine + GenStudio brand checks inside the same workflow |

**Implication for Jigi:** We are not inventing a category. We are opening the category to the mid-market and to independent agencies — with a product that is sharp, affordable, and built around the agency↔brand handoff rather than media buying or consumer ID graphs.

### Business-model learnings applied (from that research)

Holding companies do not monetise “pretty generations.” Their commercial patterns, and how Jigi mirrors them:

| Learning from best-in-class | Network evidence | How Jigi applies it |
|----------------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Sell the governed loop, not the model** | Omni, Bodhi, Open Pro, Zoyumi all bundle create → check → approve | SKU = seats + brands + handoff; generation is metered COGS |
| **Brand / compliance gate is a named product** | WPP Brand Guardian; Workfront AI Reviewer; Sapient Visual Brand Compliance Agent | Pillar D “Brand Guardian” — productise checks already in-app (see evolution note) |
| **Human oversight is non-negotiable in the pitch** | Omnicom “amplified, not automated”; curative AI language at WPP | Guest Decide + submit/review APIs remain the system of record |
| **Open the platform to brands, not only agencies** | WPP Open Pro (brands self-serve beside managed service) | Dual packaging: Agency + Brand plans; guest Decide without full SaaS |
| **Always-on / performance creative is the volume wedge** | Dentsu Zoyumi (&lt;30 min on-brand assets, scoring + production) | Generation Studio tiers + handoff OS for social/display/CRM cadence |
| **Enterprise buys stack + services; mid-market buys SaaS** | Accenture Song / Adobe programmes vs Zoyumi SaaS | Refuse Fortune-100 transformation; PLG Studio + sales-assisted Agency |
| **Do not compete with media / identity moats** | Omni Acxiom IDs; GroupM / WPP Media | Explicit non-goals year one |
| **Outcome metrics in the commercial story** | Faster production %, review-time cuts | North-star: hours candidate → decision; first-pass approval rate |
| **Partner the heavy stack later** | Havas/Omnicom on Adobe Workfront–AEM | Export/DAM path later; be the handoff layer now |

**Deliberately not copied:** retainer-led transformation, media buying, consumer ID graphs, AEM replacement, regulated MLR suites (roadmap add-on only).

### Evolution, not revolution (product stance)

Jigi already ships the industry loop in software form (Handoff OS P1–P4 merged; Generation Studio P1–P4 on the studio branch; brand checklist / OnBrandCheck / colour compliance already in Decide and review). This strategy does **not** call for a rewrite. It calls for:

1. **Commercial clarity** — name and package what exists (handoff OS, brand confidence, studio).  
2. **Deepening** — make brand checks a consistent soft-gate (evolve existing compliance surfaces).  
3. **Engine evolution** — add fal as a provider lane; keep the router.  
4. **GTM surfaces** — pricing and narrative; not a new product category.

Revolution would be throwing away in-app review for Slack, or rebuilding as a pure gen studio. We refuse that.

### What buyers are tired of

- Point tools that generate beautiful images and leave approval to email  
- Proofing tools that never knew the brand existed  
- Enterprise suites that take a year and a transformation partner to stand up  
- “AI creative” demos that cannot survive legal, brand, or channel rules

## 4. Who we serve

### Primary ICP — Agency creative studios (wedge)

**Profile**
- Independent or network-affiliated studios, ~5–80 people  
- Serving multiple brands with always-on social, display, CRM, and campaign bursts  
- Today: Figma + Drive + Slack + email + occasional Midjourney / ChatGPT  
- Pain: brand feedback is slow, ambiguous, and unversioned; juniors generate off-brand work; partners cannot show clients a clean “approved” state  

**Jobs to be done**
1. Produce more variants without losing the brand  
2. Get a client decision without chasing  
3. Prove what was approved, when, and by whom  

**Buying triggers**
- Lost pitch or delayed launch due to approval chaos  
- New brand win that needs a repeatable process  
- Client asking “where is the latest approved file?” for the tenth time  

**Success for them**
- Median hours from first candidate → first client decision ↓  
- First-pass approval rate ↑  
- Fewer Slack archaeology sessions

**Profile**
- Brand teams without Workfront / GenStudio / AEM programmes

**Jobs to be done**
1. Protect brand equity while saying yes faster  
2. Give agencies a single place to submit  
3. Keep an audit trail for legal / leadership

Technology partners (DAM, CMS, design tools), boutique consultancies, and regional agency networks who need a governed creative layer for clients who will not buy a holding-company platform.

**Partner value:** White-label or referral path into a product that already encodes handoff, roles, and brand checks — without building it.

## 6. Product pillars

### Pillar A — Brand memory

Brand kits that actually drive creation and review: voice, colours, guidelines, channel constraints, presets. Dual journey: **brand-first** when the kit is ready; **idea-first** when speed matters and brand enrichment follows.

### Pillar B — Creative production

Concepts, copy, and images (upload first-class alongside generate). Cost-aware tiers, remix, upscale, channel aspects. Engine stays swappable; quality and price improve without rewriting the product.

### Pillar C — Handoff & decide

Submit → queue → asset-first review → approve / changes / decline. Guest Decide for clients who will not create accounts. Rounds, comments, nudges, audit history. Human status language for creators and clients.

### Pillar D — Brand governance (the commercial distinctive)

Machine checks *before* and *beside* human review: palette, voice, channel rules, compliance score on the Decide screen. “Curative AI” — flag → fix → decide — not replace the brand lead.

**Already in product (evolve, don’t replace):** `OnBrandCheckPanel`, `buildBrandChecklist`, `compliance_check`, colour compliance on assets, Decide-time brand kit level. Next step is consistency, soft-gate on submit, and a client-facing name (“Brand Guardian”) — not a greenfield module that ignores these.

### Pillar E — Delivery

Approved library per campaign; export/download; clear ownership of “this is the yes.” Path later to DAM/CMS — not year-one scope.

| **Studio** | Small agency / freelancers | Limited brands, seats, generations; full handoff | Land |

Generation compute is **metered COGS** (credits or included pools), not the headline SKU. We sell **approved creative velocity**, not tokens.

### Pricing philosophy

Price against outcomes holding companies use in pitches:
- Hours saved in review chase  
- Rounds avoided  
- Cost per approved variant  

Do not lead with model names or “unlimited AI images.”

### Sales motion (year one)

1. **Product-led** for Studio (self-serve + guided onboarding)  
2. **Sales-assisted** for Agency / Brand (demo = generate → guest Decide in one session)  
3. **Partner-sourced** for regional networks  

### Expansion

More brands → more seats → higher generation pools → brand governance add-ons (regulated packs) → integrations (DAM, Slack notify) → video animate-from-approved.

## 10. Investment thesis

### Why now

1. **Category pull:** Holding companies have educated the market that governance + human yes is mandatory.  
2. **Tooling gap:** Mid-market still lives in email while gen-AI floods their folders.  
3. **Product readiness:** Jigi already has campaigns, generation, submit/review APIs, Handoff OS (guest Decide, rounds, compliance-on-Decide), dual journey, and a generation studio roadmap — not a slideware thesis.  
4. **Engine leverage:** Inference is becoming a commodity substrate (fal / Comfy / APIs). Value accrues to the workflow OS above it.

### Why Jigi

- Clear wedge ICP and refusal of holding-company theatre  
- Dual-sided usage (agency creates, brand decides) builds retention and network effects inside each account  
- Defensible data: brand kits, decision history, what gets approved — improves generation and checks over time  
- Capital-efficient: SaaS + metered compute; no GPU fleet as core business  

### Risks (honest)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Adobe / holding platforms move downmarket | Speed, price, guest Decide UX, mid-market GTM; partner not replace |
| Gen-AI studios add weak “share links” | Double down on brand governance + audit + roles |
| Model retirement / provider churn | Registry + router architecture; fal as primary substrate |
| Approval culture resists software | Guest Decide &lt;3 clicks; Filestage-class proofing |

### Use of capital (indicative)

Product depth on brand governance · engine reliability · GTM for Agency ICP · design system polish · selective integrations — not a media platform or identity graph.
